# AMR DevKit/Perceptor

This is our first AMR session in this promgram. We will introduce two ISP projects: AMR DevKit and AMR Perceptor.

## Materials & Preparation

- Receive AMR upper part, lower part, and battery from ACL
- You will find the electric screwdriver, mini screwdriver (flathead), and USB extension cable useful:
  - Electric screwdriver will be helpful for fastening a large number of screws.
  - Mini screwdriver will be required for connecting battery signal wires on AMR.
  - USB extension cable will be useful for extending USB cable connecting from lower part to upper part.
